## Youth season rule (user, 2026-10-22, from the Santa Clara batch)

A youth placement takes the first season at the youth club in which he is 17 or
18 on 1 July (no longer the first season at 14+). With no such season, the one
closest to 17-18. Missing FM26 birth dates come from Transfermarkt.

## Peak season rule for players found by name (user, 2026-10-22, Santa Clara batch)

Among his seasons at the club:
1. Only seasons with at least half of that league season's games (the club's
   "played" in FM26 league history, kept in league_played.json; year there =
   season END year). If none qualify, all seasons.
2. Prefer the athletic peak, age 23-30 on 1 July, when any season qualifies.
3. Then the highest division (competition reputation), then most league games,
   then more goals, then the earlier season.
Examples: Serginho 2013 (Segunda, 41/42) over 2018 (Primeira, 7/34); Kali 2002/03.
