FMPT Legends bookmarklets

Drag each button to your bookmarks bar.

FMPT zerozero FMPT bdfutbol

Use

  1. Open a player's page: www.zerozero.pt/jogador/... or www.bdfutbol.com/es/j/...
  2. Click the matching bookmark. It saves zerozero_<id>.txt or bdfutbol_<id>.txt to your Downloads.
  3. Run /usr/bin/python3 creator/create_legends.py sheet.csv --import-downloads: the files move into creator/cache/ and the batch reads them.

A page with several career tables (veterans, futsal, coaching) is read as the batch expects; those rows are dropped by the parser, not the bookmark.
